# Sports archive

Sports records are a fixed historical archive. Publish archive material as newsroom Pages with stable URLs. Keep historical game, team, and season redirects pointed at the corresponding archive page when it exists; the archive is separate from the editorial Sports section.

The public build verifies that generated redirects point at pages in the export. If the archive has not been published, the general Sports link goes to current Sports coverage. There is no game, roster, or team management in the newsroom.
